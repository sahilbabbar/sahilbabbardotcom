About

Sahil Babbar was introduced to computers at an early age, from playing PC games to testing his mom's GUI-based softwares, figuring out his father's palm top, to playing with film roll cameras and so on. So it was a natural learning process and impact of the upbringing that influenced his thought process.

As he graduated from high-school, he got introduced to CSS frameworks using which he created his school's website and from there his affinity towards user-interface design and development ignited.

His under-graduation introduced him to a plethora of fields in computer science and he struck a chord with Software engineering, DevOps, Security, Philosophy, UX Design and Entrepreneurship. This was the influence of an exceptional educational incubation and company of brilliant individuals.

Today he works at the cross-section of frontend development, UX design, software architecture and product design; in the enterprise software research and development domain. He likes to craft products that impact lives positively.

"Those are the fortunate ones, whose work & passion coincide."
